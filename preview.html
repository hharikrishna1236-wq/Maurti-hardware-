About Us

Creating Better Spaces with Quality Hardware.

Maruti Hardware, based in Chandni Chowk, Kolkata, offers a wide range of interior and furniture hardware for homes, designers, carpenters and fit-out professionals.

Our range spans kitchen fittings, modular kitchen accessories, wardrobe and furniture fittings, handles and profiles, hinges and drawer systems, door locks and architectural hardware.

We focus on helping you find solutions that suit your kitchen, wardrobe, furniture or architectural interior.
